# Game-Theoretic Thinker — Academic Sources

Sources grounding this thinker in academic tradition.

## 1. Theory of Games and Economic Behavior

**von Neumann, J. & Morgenstern, O.** (n.d.)

DOI: [https://doi.org/10.2307/j.ctt1b9x1w4](https://doi.org/10.2307/j.ctt1b9x1w4)

Foundational text establishing game theory as a formal discipline

## 2. The Strategy of Conflict

**Schelling, T. C.** (n.d.)

DOI: [https://doi.org/10.2307/2551195](https://doi.org/10.2307/2551195)

Applied game theory to real-world strategic behavior, commitment, and threats

## 3. The Evolution of Cooperation

**Axelrod, R.** (n.d.)

DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7466396](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7466396)

Demonstrated how cooperation emerges in repeated games via tit-for-tat
